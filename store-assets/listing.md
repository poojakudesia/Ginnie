## Full description
Dream Life turns big wishes into a daily practice you'll actually keep.

• Set up to 3 wishes and get a feasibility-aware nudge from Ginnie.
• Take the 5-question Method Match — Ginnie reads your energy and picks the 3
  practices that fit how you imagine, build habits, and where you get stuck.
• Learn each practice with guided lessons, video explainers, and an
  affirmation coach that refines what you write.
• Build a daily plan and show up on a simple day-by-day tracker — add proof,
  log how you feel, and keep your streak.
• Earn performance badges (Awakening Energy → Master Manifestor) and do a
  weekly Energy Check that suggests quick realignments when you're low.
• Celebrate your manifestations on a beautiful wishes board.

Calm, editorial design. No noise — just faster alignment.
