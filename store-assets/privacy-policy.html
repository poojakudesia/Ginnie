Privacy Policy — Dream Life

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Dream Life ("the app", "we") is a personal manifestation-practice app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Account details — your name and email address (and, if you use social sign-in, the email your provider shares). Phone number is optional.
  • Your content — the wishes, affirmations, practice check-offs, moods, uploaded proof photos, and weekly energy check-ins you create in the app.
  • Basic usage — progress state (e.g. streaks, badges) needed to run the app.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, or device advertising IDs.

How we use it

  • To provide the core features — saving your wishes, tracking practice, and personalizing guidance.
  • To generate AI suggestions: the text of an affirmation you ask to refine, and your quiz answers for practice matching, are sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce a response. This text is used only to generate your result and is not used to train models.
  • To authenticate you (secure sign-in tokens).

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We share data only with service providers that run the app on our behalf — our cloud host/database and the AI provider named above — solely to deliver the features you request.

Data storage & security

Your data is stored on secured cloud infrastructure and transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text.

Your choices & rights

  • You can edit or remove your wishes and content in the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us (below).

Children

Dream Life is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
